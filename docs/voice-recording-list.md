# Voice recording list

The voice sounds RowingMod needs, for recording on an iPhone (Voice Memos). Make **one memo per row** and leave about **1 second of silence between takes**; the takes are split automatically. Record a set per voice: male, and female if someone can.

## Before you record

- **Settings → Apps → Voice Memos → Audio Quality → Lossless.** Leave "Enhance Recording" off.
- **A quiet, soft room**, away from fans, the fridge and tiled rooms.
- **The phone 20–30 cm from your mouth, a bit to the side**, not straight in front.
- **Really make the effort:** pull a towel, push a doorframe. Acted grunts sound fake.
- **Vary every take:** vowel ("hnh", "hup", "uhh", "hah"), length and strength.

## Needed

| # | Memo name | When the game plays it | What it sounds like | Length per take | Takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `grunt-light` | On normal strong strokes | A short, controlled effort: pulling something moderately heavy | 0.2–0.6 s | 15–20 |
| 2 | `grunt-hard` | Strokes under heavy load: into a headwind, or the crew pushing hard | A full-effort pull, as if hauling an oar against the wind | 0.3–0.8 s | 15–20 |
| 3 | `brake` | The blade digging in when you start braking | A strained, held "nnngh", bracing against something pulling away | 0.5–1.0 s | 8–10 |

## Nice to have

| # | Memo name | When the game plays it | What it sounds like | Length | Takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | `breathing` | Between strokes when your stamina is low | Real panting: do jumping jacks first, then just breathe | 30–60 s continuous | 1 |
| 5 | `tired` | Running out of stamina ("Too tired to row") | An exhausted groan or sigh | 0.5–1.5 s | 5–8 |
| 6 | `clash` | Your stroke clashes with the crew's rhythm | A short annoyed grunt or "tch", oar jarring in your hands | 0.2–0.5 s | 5–8 |

## Sending them

AirDrop the memos to the Mac (they land in `~/Downloads`), then say the file names. Each take is cleaned (trimmed, denoised, levelled, EQ'd), and varied in pitch; you listen to them and pick the keepers before anything goes into the mod.
